# Skyreach: four layout concepts

On 2026-10-03 the user said the tutorial island felt bland and lonely, and asked for four 3D layouts to choose from. Each
should make the island look alive, with a few more houses that feel like a small town, defined and better-looking grounds
for the two creatures (slimes and spiders), better dirt roads with bricks, barriers or obstacles, and flowers, bushes
and other vegetation, all in the Screen Print Wash style (`Docs/Art/ScreenPrintWash.md`).

The four concepts can be explored in the concept viewer, <https://claude.ai/artifact/NoY9cGCEDzz4yB1ZwPb49e>
(`Tools/ConceptViewer`). The viewer draws the game's own models on the island's real terrain. Use "Compare all 4" to see
one viewpoint in every concept. The user has not picked one yet.

## What every concept keeps

- The spawn farmstead: farmhouse, barn, well, coop with hens, hay, cart, two farmhands and the player start.
- The orchard, filled out to full rows, and the windmill with its fan turning.
- The target range: dummies, targets, hay bales, and sandbags and barricades for cover.
- The plateau with the ruined lookout, and past it the skiff jetty with the packet skiff moored (the way off the island).
- The pond with reeds and lily pads, birches along the creek, and the bridge.
- Lone trees and bushes over the open meadows, wildflower meadows on a lighter sward, and grass verges along the roads.

## The concepts

### 1 · Crossroads Town

The crossroads grows into a small town: a cobbled square ringed by a saloon, a store, the sheriff, the undertaker and homes, with more houses along the brick-kerbed road from the farm.

- **Town**: seven buildings face a cobbled square with the memorial, the well, the gun rack, market stalls, benches and lamps; three more homes line the farm road, each with a garden, washing and a smoking chimney.
- **Roads**: brick kerbs and lamps from the farm to the range, cobbles in the square, dirt with wheel ruts elsewhere; barricades and sandbags at the range give cover.
- **Slimes**: the Wallow, a bog in the west meadow with pools, reeds, mushrooms, a sunken cart and slime trails heading for town.
- **Spiders**: Web Hollow on the forest rise, a ring of dead trees strung with webs round two burrows, cocoons hanging from the branches.
- **Green**: oak and birch groves round the town, hedgerows by the farm road, wildflower meadows west and south, pines on the rise, reeds at the pond.
- **Life**: townsfolk at the stalls, on the porches, at the well and on the road; a sheep pasture between the farm road and the plateau; hens and farmhands at the farm.

### 2 · Main Street

One frontier street runs from the crossroads to the foot of the plateau ramp: false fronts and porches on both sides, hitching rails and lamps, a water tower, and a barricade at the far end.

- **Town**: store, saloon, sheriff and a cabin on the north side; the undertaker with his coffin shed, two cottages and the water tower on the south; back lanes with sheds, outhouses, washing and gardens.
- **Roads**: Main Street is brick with kerbs and lamps; the old roads stay dirt with ruts; a wagon, crates and sandbags block the far end as cover.
- **Slimes**: the Shallows on the pond's west shore, with pools, reeds, a rickety plank walk, a fishing shed and slime trails up the bank.
- **Spiders**: the Old Homestead in the forest: a burnt chimney, a springhouse, fallen walls and a burnt wagon, all webbed over around a burrow.
- **Green**: birches along the creek, oaks behind the street, pines on the forest rise, hedges along the back lanes, flower verges by the road.
- **Fields**: west of the farm road a hedged lane runs to the town fields: wheat, a plowed field, a cottage with a garden and a sheep pasture.
- **Life**: townsfolk on the porches and in the street, farmers in the fields, hens and farmhands at the farm.

### 3 · Hill Farms

No town, but four homesteads tied together by lanes between dry-stone walls: wheat, plowed fields and pasture in a patchwork, hedgerows, and a well and a market stall at the crossroads.

- **Homesteads**: the spawn farm and its hay; Cross Farm's cabin with a walled paddock; the Mill Cottage under the windmill; the Pond Farm with a farmhouse, barn, coop and haystacks.
- **Roads**: lanes sunk between dry-stone walls, rail fences round every field, a signpost, a well and a stall where the lanes cross; stone walls and hay give cover everywhere.
- **Slimes**: the Bog Field, a flooded low field with a broken sluice gate, pools in the furrows and a fence they came through.
- **Spiders**: the Cliff Dens at the plateau's foot, webs across the rock, burrows in the scree and cocoons on the dead trees.
- **Green**: hedgerows along the walls, lone oaks in the pastures, the orchard, birches at the pond, flower margins between field and lane.
- **Life**: sheep in Cross Farm's paddock, cows in a pasture under the plateau, hens at the Pond Farm and the farmstead, farmers in the fields and at the stall.

### 4 · The Hollow Way

A wooded island. One sunken lane winds through glades of oak, birch and pine to a woodcutters' hamlet round a campfire; fallen logs, boulders and cairns mark the way and give cover.

- **Hamlet**: a cottage, a cabin and a farmhouse round a clearing, with the well, the gun rack, a campfire and benches, cordwood stacks, a log yard with stumps, a shed and lamps.
- **Roads**: dirt lanes winding between the trees, fallen logs and boulders as barriers, cairns as waymarkers, the creek bridge kept.
- **Slimes**: Mossy Spring, a spring pool by the pond among mossy boulders, reeds and mushrooms, with a hunter's camp beside it.
- **Spiders**: the Pine Thicket, where the forest road runs between dead pines strung with webs right across the way.
- **Green**: woods over most of the island, with glades for the farm, the hamlet, the range and a flower meadow; bushes at every edge.
- **Life**: woodcutters at the log yard and the firewood, folk at the well and the campfire, hens and farmhands at the farm.

## Creature grounds

| Concept | Slimes (5) | Spiders (8) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · Crossroads Town | the Wallow, a bog in the west meadow | Web Hollow, on the forest rise |
| 2 · Main Street | the Shallows, on the pond's west shore | the Old Homestead, in the forest |
| 3 · Hill Farms | the Bog Field, a flooded field with a sluice | the Cliff Dens, under the plateau's north cliff |
| 4 · The Hollow Way | Mossy Spring, beside the pond | the Pine Thicket, across the forest road |

Every slime ground is wet: a darker bog sward, pools, reeds, mushrooms and glistening trails leading away from it. Every
spider ground is dry: a needle-strewn clearing, dead trees with webs strung between them, cocoons, egg sacs and burrows
with sheet webs.

## Building the chosen concept

- **New models.** The viewer draws these from its procedural kit, and none of them is a game model yet: oak, birch, pine,
  dead pine and apple trees; bushes and hedges; flowers, grass tufts, wheat, cabbages, reeds and mushrooms; picket
  fences, barricades and sandbags; market stalls, sheds, coops, haystacks and scarecrows; cobbles and brick kerbs;
  burrows, egg sacs, cocoons, webs and slime trails; sheep, cows, hens and townsfolk. Each needs a real model made by the
  rules in `Art/README.md`.
- **Placement.** The layout moves into `Art/Levels/TutorialIsland/layout.json` and the build scripts
  (`build_tutorial_island.py`, `build_island_scatter.py`). The viewer's `concepts.js` has every position in Unreal
  centimeters.
- **Bestiary.** Livestock and townsfolk that become actors each need a bestiary page (`UBestiaryEntry`).
- **Performance.** The town concepts add buildings, and the Hollow Way adds a great many trees. Measure the chosen one
  with `perf.ps1` and `tour.ps1` against the budget in `Docs/TutorialIsland.md`.
