# 6. Below

> *For three hundred years, everything that fell from the islands vanished into the clouds. Now it's coming back up.*

Divers of the Lantern Guild go down into the endless cloud sea on lifelines to salvage what fell. Ten years ago,
Marlowe Fenn watched their mother's line come up cut. Now spiders are climbing out of the islands' undersides, wrecks
are drifting up crusted with orange crystal, and the dead are walking out of the clouds. One evening, so does
Marlowe's mother.

## Cold open

Ten years ago, on the old diving gantry at the edge of Skyreach's plateau. A child holds a lantern. A woman in a
brass diving rig kneels, kisses the child's forehead, clips on her lifeline and steps off into white nothing. The winch
pays out line, and pays out, and pays out, for a long time. Then the line goes slack and the winch spins free. The cut
end comes up wet and glowing faintly orange. The child's lantern goes out.

Today: Low Fenn, grown, wakes on the farm to the sound of their grandfather coughing.

## The world

- **The Shroud.** The islands float above a white sea of cloud with no known bottom. Whatever falls is gone. Folk say
  "gone to the Shroud" the way others say "passed on."
- **The Lantern Guild** dives anyway: brass rigs, lanterns, and lifelines run from gantries on the islands' rims,
  winched by a partner who keeps the line. A few hundred meters down, things that fell rest on the **cloudcrust**, a
  layer of cloud stiff enough to stand on, and that's **the Fallfield**: three centuries of wrecks and lost things,
  and the Guild's living.
- **The Hush.** The deeper you go, the quieter it gets. Sound dies. Compasses turn. Time slips, and you hear voices
  you know. Divers who stay too long come back hushed, or don't come back.
- **The Rising.** Lately things come *up*. Spiders climb out of the islands' undersides. Wrecks drift up, crusted with
  glowing orange crystal, **the Bloom**. And people who fell years ago walk up out of the clouds, unchanged in face and
  wrong in manner, with orange light behind their eyes: **the Returned**.
- **The secret.** At the bottom of the Shroud lies the old world, and in it **the Fathom**: a mind the size of a sea,
  made of living Bloom. It isn't evil. It grows toward whatever falls, holds it, and *remembers* it. The Returned are
  its memories of the fallen, rebuilt in Bloom. Long ago the Keepers lifted their mountains out of its reach, and the
  Reliquaries they left on every island are **seals**: lullabies in stone, keeping the Fathom's attention asleep. Break
  an island's seal and the Bloom reaches up beneath it, slowly, and pulls it down.

## Protagonist: Marlowe "Low" Fenn

Low is twenty-four, a Guild salvage diver, quiet, stubborn and too comfortable with risk. They grew up on the Fenn farm
with their grandfather **Zeke**, a retired diver whose lungs the Hush ruined, and they dive deeper than they should,
every time, hoping to find any trace of their mother.

The arc is grief. It starts as searching for the past and ends as letting it go. The story's hardest question is put
to Low directly: if something walks up out of the clouds with your mother's face, voice and memories, and loves you,
is it her?

> *"Everybody says she's gone. Everybody's never been down there."*

## Antagonist: Father Abelard Quaile

Quaile was a Guild master once, the best diver of his day after Imogen Fenn. He lost his wife and daughters when their
island fell, and went down after them farther than anyone but Imogen. He came back hushed, and then he came back
*preaching*. He leads **the Sounding**, a movement that believes the Shroud is not death but home. Down there nothing is
ever lost, only remembered, and the Returned are the proof. So every island should go down, and everyone would be
together again.

He's sincere, gentle and magnetic, and he's breaking the Keeper seals one island at a time. He doesn't care that the
Returned are copies: *"What is a person but what they remember?"* What he wants in the end is to become the Fathom's
**Speaker**, its voice above, and lead the whole Reaches down.

> *"There is no death below, child. Only remembering. Why would you climb back up to forget?"*

### Imogen Fenn

The heart of the story. The greatest diver of her generation, she made the deepest dive ever, the Long Sounding, and
her line came up cut. She walks out of the clouds at the end of the tutorial, warm, funny, exactly herself, except
that she doesn't blink, she talks about the bottom as if it were beautiful, and the Returned step aside for her. She
believes the Fathom made her, loves her, and wants to show everyone. She loves Low more. Across the story she goes from
ambiguous (sometimes an enemy, sometimes a guide) to the one who makes the final choice.

> *"Low? Oh, look at you. You got tall."*

## Enemies and creatures

- **The Sounding**: cultists in diving suits with glass helmets full of orange light, an unmistakable silhouette.
  Acolytes with rifles, **Plummets** who drop on you from above on lines, **Cantors** whose singing strengthens the
  Returned and scrambles your HUD, Anchors (chained, shielded heavies), and **Cutters** who saw through bridges and
  moorings.
- **The Returned**: the fallen of every era, rebuilt: miners, soldiers, farmers, dressed as they were when they fell.
  Many are eerily polite until provoked. **Bloomed** Returned are overgrown with crystal: slow, huge, and they burst
  into shards.
- **Wreckers**: human salvage raiders competing with the Guild in the Fallfield.
- **Creatures**:
  - Meadow Wolf spiders, driven up through the islands.
  - MossBacks in the dripping undersides.
  - Cliff Huntsmen clinging upside down to the islands' bellies.
  - Ember Tarantulas crusted with glowing Bloom.
  - Sky Jumpers that come out of the fog.
  - The deep fauna: **Lures** (anglerfish-like fliers that mimic a diver's lantern), **Fathom Eels**, **Glass
    Mantas**, and **Hushmoths**, swarms that swallow sound.
- **Bosses**: **Mother Lure** under Lanternhold, Dagny Sorrel in Lowtown, **the Choir** (a knot of singing Returned) at
  Saint Ebba's, and Quaile in the Throat.

## Skyreach in this story

1. **Move.** Low's morning on the Fenn farm; Zeke coughing on the porch.
2. **Follow the road to the village.** The Crossing's divers, winches and talk of things coming up.
3. **The gun rack.** The Guild's **fendgun**: every diver must carry one. Low takes the village's.
4. **The dummies.** Practice on the range under the windmill.
5. **The spiders.** The woods past the pond. *"They're coming up from below. Spiders never come up."*
6. **The loadout.** Check the gun before the evening dive.
7. **The lookout.** The ruined lookout is the Fenn family's old diving gantry, where Imogen made her last dive. At dusk
   Low goes up to watch the clouds, as they do every night. Far below, the Shroud glows orange. Something is climbing
   the gantry's old dive line. A woman in a brass rig steps onto the boards, dripping cloud. *"Low? You got tall."*

## Plot

### Act I: The Rising (levels 1–15)

1. The tutorial, and Imogen on the gantry.
2. **The first night.** The Returned climb Skyreach's underside and come over the plateau's edge. Low defends the
   farm. The Returned part around Imogen as if she weren't there.
3. **Lanternhold.** Guildmistress Brask wants Imogen locked up and studied. Low hides her on the farm instead. The
   Guild's reports are grim: islands are dropping, with Bloom roots on their undersides, and the Sounding is smashing
   Reliquaries.
4. **The Undersides.** Chase Sounding Cutters beneath Lanternhold through the roots and the upside-down Keeper halls.
   Mother Lure has been luring divers off their lines for years.
5. **Brightwater falls.** A neighboring island's seal is broken. Evacuate it while orange roots drag it down: a timed
   level on a tilting island.
6. Imogen is gone in the morning. Her note: *"I have to show them the way home. Come find me, Low. Come down."*

### Act II: The Descent (levels 15–35)

7. **The Fallfield.** Low's first truly deep dive, with Tub on the line. Lowtown, the town that fell eighty years ago,
   lies on the cloudcrust with its lamps lit. The Wreck-Queen Dagny Sorrel claims it. In the wreck of Imogen's diving
   bell, Low finds her logbook. Its last pages are written in a steadier hand than any frightened diver's.
8. **The Hushwood.** Bloom trees, total silence, Hushmoths. Sound fails and the HUD stutters. In a Sounding camp,
   Sister Vey defects.
9. **Saint Ebba's.** Quaile preaches to the faithful and the Returned, with Imogen at his side. The Choir rises to stop
   Low. In Quaile's study, Low learns the truth: the Returned are the Fathom's memories in Bloom. Imogen is a copy, with
   every memory up to the moment she fell. Quaile shrugs. *"What is a person but what they remember?"*
10. Imogen is torn between the thing that made her and the child she remembers raising, and gets Low out.
11. **The Great Sounding.** Quaile breaks the great seal beneath Lanternhold. Bloom begins climbing toward the whole
    Rim.

### Act III: The Floor (levels 35–50)

12. **The Floor.** Low goes all the way down, past every record. Down there is the old world, dark and enormous, lit
    by pools of Bloom, with the Keepers' cities in ruins. Their carvings explain everything: the Fathom isn't hungry.
    It's lonely. It reaches for whatever falls because it has never been able to keep anything, and the Keepers sang it
    to sleep and lifted their homes away.
13. **The Throat.** Down into the Fathom itself, through rooms built from three centuries of memories. Quaile has
    given himself to the Bloom and become **the Speaker**, crowned in crystal on a throne of fallen things. The final
    fight.

### Ending

Quaile is gone, but the Fathom is fully awake and reaching. No new seal will hold it. Someone has to go in and *talk*
to it: teach it what letting go means. Imogen takes Low's hand. *"I already came back once. That's more than most
people get."* She walks into the light.

The Bloom recedes. The islands steady. Tub hauls Low up the longest line the Guild has ever run. Epilogue: Lanternhold
at dusk, Low on the gantry. Far below, the clouds glow a faint, slow orange, like breathing.

## Side quests (samples)

- **Hollis.** A Returned boy looking for parents who died thirty years ago. They fell too, and they're in the
  Fallfield.
- **The Record.** Beat Imogen's old depth records at every layer. Her times are carved into the Guild's board.
- **The Bell.** A diving bell lost for a week still has a heartbeat on its line. Get them out before the air runs out.
- **Grandpa's Last Dive.** Zeke wants to go down one last time, and wants Low on his line.

## Loot in this world

- **Who makes the guns.** Everything that falls comes back eventually, so the Fallfield is full of three centuries of
  guns: old Homestead, Regulator and Coachman pieces, rebuilt by Guild armorers. The Guild makes its own Kestrel dive
  carbines and Breacher close-quarters shotguns; Scrapjack is the Wreckers' brand.
- **Rarity and the beams.** Rarity is **depth**: the deeper a gun lay, the more Bloom it soaked up, and Bloom shines.
  The loot beam is that light leaking out of salvage.
- **Chests.** Supply Crates are Guild caches along dive lines. Strongboxes are safes from Lowtown's fallen bank.
  Reliquaries are Keeper seals; only spent ones, whose song has gone quiet, are safe to open, and each holds a Keeper
  weapon.
- **Elements (when they come).** **Bloom** (crystallizes a target, then shatters it), **Hush** (silences: no
  abilities, no calls for help), **Lantern** (burning light, strongest against the Returned) and **Pressure** (an
  implosion).

## Endgame (levels 50–70)

The strongest endgame of the eight:

- **Dives.** The Shroud never ends. A dive is a descent assembled from layer kits (Fallfield, Hushwood, Floor), and
  depth is the difficulty. Your **lantern oil** is the clock, and **the Hush** builds as modifiers the deeper you go.
  You choose when to turn back. Deeper loot is better, but you only keep it if you make it back up the line. Die
  below, and your haul waits at the end of your line, to be recovered once.
- **Rising Tides.** The Returned climb an island; hold it until dawn.
- **Lost Divers.** Rescue named divers, each a short story.
- **The Depth Board.** The Guild's record board: personal bests and records.

## Production notes

- **Reuses:** the island art kit, heavily. Lowtown and the Fallfield are the tutorial's own houses, barns and fences,
  tilted, broken and dusted with Bloom. The waterfall becomes the Spill, the divers' guide line. Lantern posts become
  dive anchors.
- **New:** an underside rock kit (stalactites, hanging roots); cloud floors made of cheap height fog and painted layers
  (volumetric clouds cost 9.5 ms on Medium, so they're out); a Bloom crystal kit as an emissive surface (Nanite-safe);
  the Hush (audio and a post effect, measured); and for the endgame, a dive builder that stitches room kits together,
  the biggest tech item.
- **Risk:** medium. Horror needs restraint, and dark, foggy scenes still have to hold 8.3 ms at every viewpoint.

## Why pick it, and what to watch

Pick it for the strongest endgame loop here (dives scale forever and the fiction explains every rule), a mystery deep
enough for a long game, a personal emotional core, and the heaviest reuse of the island kit. Watch out: horror turns
some players away (keep the top bright and the dread slow), fog and darkness are easy to make expensive, and *Made in
Abyss* is the obvious comparison. Going down is the shared idea, but a mother who comes back up isn't in that story.
