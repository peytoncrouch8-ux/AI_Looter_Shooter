# The Gilded Lily: area 2 (design only; NOT to build until the user says)

Story canon: `Docs/Story.md` (Ned Purcell, Odessa Lark, levels 8-13, "The Lantern Leans"). This file holds the user's
design answers of 2026-10-28 (tap answers, grill round) and Main's open questions. The user: "I don't want to make the
gilded lily yet; but I want some idea about what it is".

## The user's calls (2026-10-28)
- **The ship: a Titanic-like grand ocean liner on water, NOT an airship** (the user: "I don't want an airship, the lily
  should be a titanic like ship on water"). Overrides Story.md decision 15a (airship over the canyon at Highwater
  Landing). Gone with it: the canyon tether, gondolas, rigging, the falls fight, the Sky Jumpers' rigging home.
- **Where:** anchored out at sea off a port town at the end of the rail line. Reached by the Lily's launch.
- **Boarding:** win a seat at Ned's table: earn a gilded marker in the port (missions, bounties, a rigged card game);
  then the Lily's launch rows the player out.
- **Size: bigger than Ransom's Rest** (RR: valley ~42,800 m2 + mountain ~50% = ~64,000 m2). Main's working number
  (unconfirmed): shore ~1.5x RR (~95,000 m2) plus the ship's decks.
- **Layout: town first, ship last.** The shore is most of the map (missions, camps, loot); boarding the Lily is the climax.
- **Shore districts (all four):** the port town (station, saloons, hotel, bank, Saint Hester's chapel, harbor master);
  docks and shipyards (cranes, warehouses, dry docks, fish market, coal bunkers feeding the liner); sea cliffs and a
  lighthouse (headland, coves, wrecks, caves); beaches and tide flats (wreck camps, smugglers' coves, tide pools).
- **Ship decks played:** casino floor and ballroom (grand staircase, tables, Ned's fight); promenade and upper decks
  (open decks, lifeboats, four funnels, the bridge, sea wind); engine room and hold (boilers, coal bunkers, cargo hold,
  the house's vault of grave gold). Not cabins/suites.
- **Mood: gilded and rotten:** gold, red velvet and brass lights over the sea, the glamour peeling, the house going
  Hollow with Ned; warm sunset and night lights.
- **Enemies:** Ned's crew going Hollow (card sharps, bouncers, croupiers: the game's first gunfights against people) on
  shore and ship; **shore creatures** (new wildlife: e.g. giant crabs on the beaches, eels in tide pools, gulls on the
  cliffs; kinds to design). Not picked: the drowned dead, smugglers/wreckers, Sky Jumpers, the Unpaid's return.
- **Ned's fight: a rigged card game** in the ballroom: each phase Ned deals a card that changes the rules (ricochet
  rounds, he heals, double crits for the player); his bullets ricochet off the gilded walls.
- **The ship stays afloat** after Ned (no sinking); revisitable, its casino still running.
- **Unlocks:** casino slot machines (Borderlands-like: cash a pull; ammo, cash or a gun; a rare jackpot legendary; open
  after Ned); Ozias's gunsmith joins the hub after the Lily. Not picked: Ned's Fortune power as a reward, a new gun
  kind/maker.
- **Part swapping first appears with Ozias** (the user: "Gun parts bench shouldn't be unlocked until the lily"; "Lock it
  now"): Ransom Farm's gunsmith's bench is taken out of Ransom's Rest now (2026-10-28).
- **Skyreach's link: decide later** (was a skylift from the airship's north side).
