# 1. The Lodestone War

> *They came for the stone that holds our island in the sky. We'll make them pay for every pound.*

A railroad baron is mining the lodestone that keeps the frontier islands in the air, and every island his Company
strips sinks into the clouds. Rowan Hale came to Skyreach to claim a dead aunt's farm. They end up leading the Rim's
homesteaders in a range war for the sky itself.

## Cold open

Black screen. An old man's voice, courteous and unhurried: *"When I was nine years old, my island fell."* A boy
clings to a glowing orange stone in an endless white sea of cloud. The light changes from noon to night to noon again.
A mail skiff's horn sounds somewhere far off. Cut to sixty years later: the same stone sits under a glass dome on a
desk in Concord, and Augustin Vane signs a map of the Rim with a fountain pen. Cut to a mail skiff dropping out of the
clouds onto Skyreach's little dock, a passenger asleep on the mail sacks. The pilot kicks their boot. *"Wake up,
Hale. You're home."*

## The world

- **The Reaches** are hundreds of islands drifting above **the Shroud**, a sea of cloud with no known bottom. The old,
  rich islands near the middle are the Inner Reaches, with the capital, **Concord**. The outer islands are **the Rim**,
  opened to homesteaders forty years ago by the **Homestead Charter**: work a claim for five years and it's yours.
  Skyreach is a Rim island, about as far out as anyone farms.
- **Lodestone** keeps the islands up. It's an amber-orange crystal that weighs less than nothing: it falls upward.
  Every island has a lodestone heart, with roots running through the rock like veins. Cut lodestone lifts airships,
  holds up cable cars and sets the price of land.
- Nobody knows who raised the islands. They left traces that folks call **Keeper** work: stone wells that go down
  forever, ruined watchtowers, and **Reliquaries**, granite chests banded in gold with orange rune-lines and a chip of
  lodestone hovering over the lid.
- The **Vane Lodestone & Rail Company** holds the charter to build **the Skyline**, a railway hung on cables between
  islands from lodestone pylons, which will tie the Rim to Concord. It also holds the mineral rights under every Rim
  island, in a clause no homesteader ever read.
- **The secret nobody knows they know.** Lodestone is alive, in a slow mineral way, and it grows when it's watered. The
  Keeper wells carry rainwater down to the heart, and the windmill pumps the old-timers keep turning "because grandpa
  did" are what keep their islands up. Cut out the heart and sever the roots, and an island sinks a hand's breadth a
  year, then all at once. The Rim calls the slow sinking **the Settling** and blames the weather.

## Protagonist: Rowan Hale

Rowan grew up in Concord's tenements and spent ten years on the Skyline crews: climbing cables, setting charges,
laying track over nothing. When a pylon gave way and killed their crew, the Company blamed the dead men for it and
docked Rowan's pay for the lost tools. Rowan quit with nothing. A month later a letter came: Aunt Odessa, whom they'd
met twice, had died and left them the Hale homestead on Skyreach.

Rowan arrives meaning to keep their head down, grow potatoes and never think about the Company again. The arc goes
from Concord's lesson, *the sky doesn't care about you*, to the Rim's, *we hold each other up*: a reluctant neighbor
becomes a reluctant leader. Their past is useful. They know how the Company builds, where a pylon is weak and how a
train is guarded, and they climb cables and handle explosives like other people tie their boots.

> *"I came out here to grow potatoes. Nobody told me the potatoes would need defending."*

## Antagonist: Augustin Vane

Vane is sixty-eight, the president of the Company and the richest man in the Reaches. He was born on **Little Calder**,
a Rim island that sank when he was nine. His parents and sister went down with it. He survived by clinging to a chunk
of lodestone in the Shroud for two days until a mail skiff found him. The lesson he took has run his whole life: the
sky doesn't care, only stone keeps you up, and whoever owns the stone owns the sky.

He built the Company from a single salvage barge. He is brilliant, patient, personally kind (he funds orphanages in
Concord) and completely certain. He believes the Settling will drop every island in the Reaches within two
generations, and his answer is **the Firmament**: Concord rebuilt on a mountain of harvested lodestone, a city that can
never fall. Whoever is inside will live. He calls it a lifeboat: *"I can't save everyone. I can save a city."*

What he hides: his own geologist, Dr. Wendell Pell, proved the mining *causes* the Settling and that lodestone can be
regrown. Regrowing takes decades and can't be owned, and the Firmament needs stone now. Vane buried the **Pell Report**.
Pell died in an accident a month later. Vane never asked his Chief Regulator how.

> *"Your aunt was a fine woman. She believed the ground loved her back. The ground, Hale, does not love anyone."*

## Enemies and creatures

- **The Cinder Gang**: raiders paid to burn. Firebugs throw bottles of lamp oil, Torchmen carry homemade flame guns,
  and Skimmer riders come in low on lodestone sleds.
- **Company Regulators**, the main enemy army, carrying the Regulator rifle: riflemen, Breachers with shields and
  shotguns, Spotters who mark you for mortar crews, Linemen with nail guns and cable hooks, **Lifters** in lodestone
  harnesses who float down on you from above, and **Ironsides**, armored heavies with Thunderdrum shotguns.
- **The Fallen**: survivors living on the wreckage of sunk islands. Desperate rather than evil, and hostile to anyone
  from above.
- **Creatures**, driven out of the islands' undersides by the blasting:
  - Meadow Wolf spiders on the Rim's meadows, the first sign something is wrong.
  - MossBacks in the damp, overgrown undersides around the Keeper wells.
  - Cliff Huntsmen lying flat on Hollowmount's cut faces.
  - Ember Tarantulas deep in the mines, where cut lodestone runs hot: dead stone burns.
  - Sky Jumpers nesting on the Skyline's cables and pylons.
  - **Stonehogs**, boars that root for lodestone pebbles and float a moment when shot in the belly; **Gullwyrms**, pale
    cloud serpents that snatch the careless off ledges.

## Skyreach in this story

The tutorial plays as written, with the story's words:

1. **Move.** Rowan steps off Gully's skiff at the farmstead dock with a suitcase and the deed. The house is shut up.
   Odessa's grave is in the orchard, the dirt still fresh.
2. **Follow the road to the village.** The Crossing: a dozen families, a sheriff's office, a blacksmith.
3. **The gun rack.** The Crossing keeps a town rifle on the rack in the square "for whoever needs it." Marisol tells
   Rowan to take it: *"Odessa would want her kin armed. Things have been loud lately."*
4. **The dummies.** The militia's practice range under the windmill. The pump creaks. Marisol: *"Keep the wheel
   turning. Odessa used to say that."*
5. **The spiders.** Spiders have been boiling up out of the island's underside since the Company started blasting next
   door. *"They're running from something."*
6. **The loadout.** Abel Dunmore looks the rifle over and shows Rowan its parts.
7. **The lookout.** The Cinder Gang burned it last spring. From the top, Rowan watches the Company's mining rig on
   **Tallow Flats**, the neighboring island, and sees the whole island lurch and drop a dozen meters, its windmill
   toppling into the clouds. That night the Cinder Gang rides on the Crossing.

## Plot

### Act I: Claim (levels 1–15)

1. The tutorial, ending with Tallow Flats sinking.
2. **The night raid.** The Cinder Gang hits the Crossing. Rowan defends the farmhouse in waves while the barn burns.
3. Morning. Silas Pruitt arrives with a sympathetic face and an offer for the Hale claim. After he leaves, Marisol
   tells Rowan that Odessa didn't fall from the plateau by accident: she had turned Pruitt down three times.
4. Under the farmhouse floor, Rowan finds Odessa's **Settling Book**: thirty years of measured island heights. Every
   island near a Company dig sank faster than the rest. Tucked inside is her **lodestone charm**.
5. **Tallow Flats.** Rowan helps Granny June hold her homestead, fights the Cinder Gang through the tilted wheat and
   corners Matches Molloy in the burning silo. Before he dies he laughs: the Company pays by the acre burned.
6. Theo Lark deserts from the Company's survey team and brings the dig map. Skyreach is next.
7. Marisol, Granny June, Theo, Gully and Rowan found the **Rim Alliance**. Gully's skiff route becomes its network.
   The Company declares the Alliance outlaws, and the Regulators come.

### Act II: The Line (levels 15–38)

8. **The Skyline.** The Alliance robs Company trains for rifles, payroll and paperwork: train jobs across trestles
   over the clouds. Rowan knows these trains; they helped build the line. The Starling twins fight them on the roof of
   a moving train.
9. **Hollowmount.** The miners are paid in Company scrip and kept in debt. Big Ida Stroud fights Rowan from the Bore
   Mule while the floor gives way. If Rowan saves her trapped miners during the fight instead of chasing the kill, Ida
   later brings her crews over to the Alliance.
10. **Tumbledown.** Odessa's book points to the islands that sank a decade ago. Rowan goes down to the Shelf and finds
    Mayor Kell and the Fallen, who want everyone above to know what falling is like. Kell grew up with Vane on Little
    Calder and survived the same fall. Two survivors of one fall: one decided to own the stone, the other to drag
    everyone down.
11. **Gallows Reach.** Marisol is captured in a raid. Rowan breaks her out of the Company jail, cracks the records
    office and steals the **Pell Report**. Chief Regulator Crowe is waiting. After the fight, his ledger has an entry
    in his neat hand: *O. Hale, the plateau, forty crowns.*
12. **The turn.** Theo decodes the report: lodestone grows when watered, the Keeper wells feed it, and the mining
    causes the Settling. *Keep the wheel turning.* Odessa knew, or guessed.
13. **The fall of Skyreach.** Vane strikes back. Ironclads shell the Crossing, sappers blow Skyreach's Keeper well and
    the windmill comes down. The island drops thirty meters in one night. The waterfall stops and the pond drains.
    It's the lowest point of the story, and the hub shows it.

### Act III: Keep the Wheel Turning (levels 38–50)

14. **The Taproot.** Rowan goes beneath the biggest Rim island to the Keeper heart. The halls show how the wells were
    built to water the stone. The Well-Wyrm, a blind white serpent that has lived in the wells for centuries, guards
    the way.
15. **Raising Skyreach.** The Alliance rebuilds the well and the windmill: Ida's miners, the Tallows, even some of the
    Fallen. The wheel turns, water runs down into the island, and Skyreach rises a hand's breadth. The waterfall
    trickles back. It's the first hope in a long time.
16. **Hesper.** Rowan sends the Pell Report to Hesper Vane under a flag of truce. She reads it, confronts her father,
    and brings the *Steadfast* over to the Rim.
17. **Concord.** The Alliance hits the capital while the Firmament's foundation rises on harvested stone, and
    Hesper's *Steadfast* duels her father's flagship, the *Providence*, over the docks. Vane isn't aboard. Company
    surveyors trailed Rowan into the Taproot, so he now knows exactly where the Rim's heart is. The **Deep Bore** has
    already left to drill it, and drilling it will drop thirty Rim islands at once.
18. **The finale.** The Alliance fleet and the *Steadfast* against the Company over the Taproot, then down into the
    halls. The Deep Bore is a colossal drilling engine with Vane at the controls, fought in phases as it grinds toward
    the heart.

### Ending

The Bore breaks apart and its rig tears loose over the Shroud with Vane on it, clinging to a chunk of lodestone exactly
as he did at nine. Rowan can reach for his hand or let go.
- **Reach**, and Vane lives to stand trial. He testifies, the Charter's mineral clause is struck down and the Company
  is broken up.
- **Let go**, and he falls with his machine, still holding the stone.

Either way, the Firmament stops rising and the harvested stone goes back. Epilogue: Skyreach in the afternoon. The
windmill turns, the waterfall runs, Gully's skiff lands with the mail. Rowan opens the Settling Book and writes this
year's height, a few meters higher than last year's.

## Side quests (samples)

- **The Widow's Well.** An old woman on a tiny island has kept her Keeper pump turning by hand for forty years. Her
  island is the only one near a dig that hasn't sunk. She thinks it's luck.
- **Scrip.** Hollowmount miners want their debts burned. Rob the Company store's ledger safe (a Strongbox) and decide
  whether to burn the books or keep them as evidence.
- **The Mail Must Go Through.** Gully is ambushed by Skimmer riders. Escort the skiff through a Gullwyrm nest.
- **Little Calder.** Find the wreck of Vane's childhood island on the Tumbledown shelf. In the ruins of his family's
  house is a child's drawing of an island with a windmill on it.

## Loot in this world

- **Who makes the guns.** Homestead Ironworks makes farm guns in walnut and iron. Regulator Arms makes the Company's
  precise blued rifles. Kestrel Aeronautic makes light bullpups for airship crews, Scrapjack rebuilds wrecks, and
  Thunderdrum Ordnance makes the Company's heavy shotguns. Rim smiths like Abel take all of them apart and put them
  back together, which is the parts system.
- **Rarity and the beams.** Every gun carries a sliver of lodestone in its action to lighten it and steady the recoil.
  When a gun leaves its dead owner's hand, its sliver flares, which is the loot beam: grey slag, then green, blue,
  purple, and orange heartstone.
- **Chests.** Supply Crates are Company supply drops along the Skyline. Strongboxes are Company payroll safes, the
  prize of every train job. Reliquaries are Keeper caches in old ruins, each holding a weapon set with heartstone.
- **Elements (when they come).** Fire from the Cinder Gang, Shock from the Company's galvanic rounds, Corrosive from
  spider venom and mine acid, and **Lift**, the setting's own: heartstone rounds make a target weightless, so it
  drifts up and, near an edge, away.

## The game's systems, explained

| System | In this story |
|---|---|
| Falling off an island | Odessa's lodestone charm won't let its wearer fall; it lifts Rowan back to solid ground. In Act III it turns out to be a seed of living lodestone, and Rowan plants it in Skyreach's rebuilt well |
| Death and respawn | Left as a game convention: Rowan wakes at the last Alliance camp |
| Loot beams | The lodestone sliver in a freed gun flares in its grade's color |
| Bestiary | The back half of Odessa's Settling Book, a naturalist's notes that Rowan keeps adding to |
| Level-ups | Plain experience |

## Endgame (levels 50–70)

- **Range War.** Company remnants and Cinder holdouts raid Alliance islands. Defend the windmills in waves, at rising
  threat tiers.
- **Train Jobs.** Repeatable heists on armored Company treasure trains with Strongbox payrolls, with modifiers such as
  armored cars, an ironclad escort or a burning trestle.
- **Deep Veins.** Mine descents under the islands that get hotter with depth (Ember Tarantulas, dead stone). The best
  heartstone is at the bottom.
- **Wanted.** Bounty posters for Company officers who fled with stone: named elites with rotating legendary drops.
- **Regrowth.** Restore Keeper wells on your claim. Each one raises its island a little and unlocks a reward.

## Signature moments

- Watching Tallow Flats sink from the lookout, the tutorial's last beat.
- A gunfight on train roofs as the train crosses a trestle over the clouds.
- The Bore Mule drilling up through the arena floor.
- Skyreach dropping thirty meters in a night, then rising again when the windmill turns.
- Hesper's *Steadfast* breaking the Company line over Concord.
- The last reach for Vane's hand above the Shroud.

## Production notes

- **Reuses:** the tutorial island as the hub (burned, sunk and rebuilt states are variants of one level); the windmill
  becomes the story's symbol; the three chests fit as they are; the Regulator, Kestrel, Homestead, Scrapjack and
  Thunderdrum designs become gunsmiths.
- **New:** a train level (cars moving along a fixed track while the world scrolls past); hub states as swappable
  sublevels; wave-defense mode; ironclads as set pieces you fight on and around, never vehicles you steer.
- **Risk:** the lowest of the eight. Everything stays in the frontier art kit already built.

## Why pick it, and what to watch

Pick it for the closest fit to what's built, a heartfelt story about people, and a hub that visibly grows and suffers
with the story. Watch out: "a western in the sky" has been done (Bioshock Infinite, Skies of Arcadia), and "sacrifice
the few to save the city" is a familiar villain. Its strength has to come from Vane's history, the lodestone secret
and the sinking islands, so lean on those.
