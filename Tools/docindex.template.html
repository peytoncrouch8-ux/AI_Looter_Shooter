Every Markdown document in the project, shelved by what it's for. The docs are written for the AI; this page is the map of them for you. Rebuilt with node Tools/docindex.js.

Keeping it organised

  1. Move working notes into git. Contracts and plans in Saved/ hold real decisions but aren't backed up. Proposal: a Docs/Work/<date>-<job>/ folder per job, moved to the archive once the job is committed.
  2. Split the long living docs. Pipeline.md (over 2,000 lines) becomes a folder of topic files with a short index; Areas/RansomsRest.md splits by sub-area.
  3. Keep the handoff short. Only "now" and "next" in Handoff.md; finished sessions go to Archive/Handoff_history.md at each session swap.
  4. Retire finished research. The Style Lab trials are over (you kept today's look): its brief, catalog and research (about 2,000 lines) can move to the archive.
  5. One map. This page and Docs/INDEX.md are rebuilt at every session end, so new files never get lost.
